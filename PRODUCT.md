# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Print shop operators, estimators, finance staff, warehouse staff, production planners, and managers use this system during daily operations. They work under time pressure, often comparing quantities, costs, stock, machine rates, deadlines, and customer commitments before deciding what to quote, produce, receive, issue, or invoice.

## Product Purpose

Printerp manages print operations from estimation through inventory, production, dispatch, finance, and reporting. Success means users can produce accurate quotes, keep materials and machines visible, reduce manual reconciliation, and move work through the shop without losing operational context.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, precise. The interface should feel trustworthy and steady, with enough density for experienced operators and enough clarity for fast decisions.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style admin screens, oversized decorative cards, loud gradients, novelty controls, dashboard clutter, and visual treatments that make operational data harder to scan. The estimator should not feel like a brochure or a toy calculator.

## Design Principles

- Keep decisions close to the data that drives them.
- Make money, material, and machine signals easy to scan.
- Prefer quiet hierarchy over decorative emphasis.
- Use familiar Filament patterns before custom UI.
- Surface missing information clearly without shaming the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible workflows. Preserve reduced-motion preferences, avoid color-only status communication, and keep dense operational views readable for long work sessions.
